# Score notation glyphs

The PNG glyphs in this folder are rasterized with ImageMagick from SVG originals on
Wikimedia Commons. The SVG sources are kept in `svg/`. Apart from trimming transparent
margins and rasterizing, the shapes are unchanged. The CC BY 2.5 files are attributed to
their author below; the full license text is at
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/legalcode>.
